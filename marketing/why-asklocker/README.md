# Why we built AskLocker: can be posted today

Three short films, each in 16:9 (YouTube, LinkedIn, the website) and 9:16
(Reels, Shorts, WhatsApp Status), with a cover for each in `covers/`. They
make no claim about encryption, so they can go out now.

In the founder's own words, emotional first, then the reasons: young people
who look after their family's papers, families who would need to find them,
and business owners whom everyone asks for papers. Each opens at night, on
the feeling, turns to day on AskLocker's name, and shows the app doing what
the story asked for.

| **Why we built AskLocker**: The founder's story. Every family has one person who handles everything; his parents always told him to keep a record of everything he does online; today everything is online, so he kept copies everywhere. Then: if I'm not there, how will they find it? So, one place where your family just asks. For your family, and for your business.
