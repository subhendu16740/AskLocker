## AskLocker for Business

The document vault for small businesses.

## The challenge

A business runs on documents: sales invoices, purchase bills, GST returns, licences and registrations, contracts and agreements, business insurance, bank and loans, and payroll. They arrive by email, on chat apps and on paper, and stay wherever they land.

## The next 90 days

Each one carries a date that matters. Arora Electric's next 90 days hold two supplier bills due, an invoice to collect, the shop insurance and the godown lease. Five dates in five documents. Missing one means late fees, a lapsed policy or an unpaid invoice.

## One place for every business document

AskLocker for Business, in five parts:

- Capture: scan or upload. AskLocker files it and reads it.
- Ask: plain questions, answered from your documents.
- Overview: renewals, bills due, money in and out.
- Reminders: alerts before a licence or policy lapses.
- Team: your team and your accountant, by invitation.

## Access and control

In AskLocker, only your team can open your records.

- Access by invitation: nobody joins unless an admin asks and they accept.
- Admins in charge: admins manage the team and can remove any record. A business always keeps one.
- Records that stay: when someone leaves, what they added stays with the business.
- Sharing with limits: share one document by link for 1 or 7 days, or 30 on a paid plan, and turn it off any time.

To answer questions, AskLocker sends passages of your documents to outside AI services. Settings › Privacy names them.
